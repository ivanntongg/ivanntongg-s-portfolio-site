Wah Kee Pet Shop is an e-commerce pet shop platform with an integrated Point of
Sale system designed for both web and mobile. The POS was built for shop
management, enabling smooth transactions, inventory tracking and order management
across retail and online channels.

## Design goals

As the UI/UX Designer, I was responsible for:

- Designing the cashier's web-based POS dashboard for desktop and tablet.
- Creating a mobile POS app for on-the-go sales.
- Ensuring both systems shared a consistent visual language, optimised workflows
  and easy-to-learn interfaces.

<Figure src={img02} alt="Visual identity applied across web and mobile POS." caption="Consistent visual identity applied to both web and mobile POS systems." />

## Key features

### Dashboard

- Real-time sales overview with key KPIs such as today's sales and top-selling items.
- Stock alerts for low or critical inventory.
- Quick access shortcuts to frequently used modules.
- Clean, prioritised layout to avoid overwhelming users.

### Open and close operation

- Logging system ensures accountability per cashier.
- Auto-generated summary report covering transactions, refunds and adjustments.
- Secure lockout after closure to prevent late edits.

<Figure src={img03} alt="POS opening procedure at the start of the day." caption="Opening the POS machine at the start of the day." />

### Hold order

- Staff can pause orders without losing data, for example when a customer forgets an item.
- Visual queue of held orders for quick retrieval.
- Improves customer service flow during peak hours.

<Figure src={img04} alt="Order management with hold and resume." caption="Order management with the ability to hold and resume." />

### Web and mobile flow alignment

- Unified design system for visual consistency.
- Identical workflows across platforms.

<Figure src={img05} alt="POS experience across web and mobile." caption="The POS experience aligned across web and mobile." />

## Outcome

The new Wah Kee POS streamlined in-store and online operations by unifying them
into a single platform.
