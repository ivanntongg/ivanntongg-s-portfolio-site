Geareach Hong Kong Ltd., a camera and lens distributor, relied heavily on
spreadsheets to manage their lens inventory, influencer collaborations, and
deal-to-contract workflows. The new Internal Data Stream Portal helps to
centralize lens inventory, influencer management, contract workflows, and
campaign tracking into one seamless platform.

## Initial challenge

Before the new system, Geareach relied heavily on manual tools like Excel
spreadsheets and Word/PDF templates. While these methods worked as a quick fix,
they created several critical issues.

  and Logistics.

## Key features

### Influencer management

- CMS for influencer profiles: name, social media, status, lenses on hand.
- Status control: Available, Occupied, Not Available, Blacklisted.
- History tracking across campaigns, reviews, loans and deals.

<Figure src={img04} alt="Table view for managing and filtering influencer records." caption="Centralized table view for managing and filtering influencer records." />

<Video src="/work/geareach/v06.mp4" width={1576} height={792}

### Lens and lens request list

- Add and remove lenses, with duplicate records prevented.
- Create new lens requests and send them to vendors.
- Vendors receive notifications and approve requests directly in the system.

### Auto contract generation

- Marketing can auto-generate contracts prefilled with lens or influencer data.
- Export as PDF or send as an e-contract by email.
- Signed contracts can be uploaded back into the portal.

### Campaigns and public workspace

- Create campaigns and tasks, and set project handlers.
- Calendar integration for tasks, meetings and reminders.
- Board and list views for project status.

The new Geareach Internal Data Stream System transformed how the marketing and

fragmented across Excel sheets and inconsistent contract templates is now
centralized, structured and automated.
